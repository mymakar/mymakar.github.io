About

I am a PhD student at the Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL) at MIT. I study machine learning, causal inference, and the intersection between the two. My work focuses on building data-efficient causal inference methods in resource-constrained settings, and building robust predictive ML models using ideas from causality.
