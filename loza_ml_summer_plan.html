🤖 Wall-Following Robot Navigation
A real robot equipped with 24 ultrasonic sensors navigates around a room. The task: classify each moment into one of 4 movement commands — move forward, turn left, turn right, or slight right. Loza will clean the data, visualize the sensor readings, train multiple classifiers, and compare their accuracy. Robots, sensors, and decision-making — directly relevant to her interests in robotics and aeronautical engineering.
